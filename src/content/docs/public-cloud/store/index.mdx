The Store is a tab in the ZCP console where an account buys services and licences. Everything you
order there bills on the same invoice as your instances, volumes and other resources. Store products
are account level. They are not tied to a project, so a support plan or a licence covers the whole
account.

## Buy a Product

- From the left-hand menu, click **Store**.
- Choose a category tab, for example Support, Professional Services or Bare Metal.
- Pick a quantity where the product allows it.
- Click the product and confirm the order.

Billing starts when the order is placed. A recurring product renews each billing cycle until you
cancel it. A one-time product bills once. Orders and their status appear under **Billing** in the
console, next to your invoices and transactions. See [Billing](/public-cloud/billing/) for how
invoices and payment methods work.

:::note

Cancelling a recurring product mid-cycle stops the next renewal. It does not credit an invoice we
have already issued.

:::

  Security Audit. $3,500 one time. We review your account, network and workload configuration once a
  year and give you written findings.

## For Software Vendors

ZSoftly lists third-party products in the Store for its customers. ZSoftly handles billing and
first-line order handling, and pays the vendor a revenue share on each sale. To list a product,
reach us through the [contact form](https://zcp.zsoftly.ca/contact?source=docs&topic=partnership)
with the partnership topic selected.
