The Store is a tab in the ZCP console where an account buys services and licences. Everything you
order there bills on the same invoice as your instances, volumes and other resources. Store products
are account level. They are not tied to a project, so a support plan or a licence covers the whole
account.

## Buy a Product

- From the left-hand menu, click **Store**.
- Choose a category tab, for example Support, Professional Services or Bare Metal.
- Pick a quantity where the product allows it.
- Click the product and confirm the order.

Billing starts when the order is placed. A recurring product renews each billing cycle until you
cancel it. A one-time product bills once. Orders and their status appear under **Billing** in the
console, next to your invoices and transactions. See [Billing](/public-cloud/billing/) for how
invoices and payment methods work.

:::note

Cancelling a recurring product mid-cycle stops the next renewal. It does not credit an invoice we
have already issued.

:::

## How purchases are approved

Products priced at CAD 100 a month or less, and one-time products of CAD 100 or less, can be bought
directly in the Store by any account.

Products above CAD 100 can be bought directly by accounts on postpaid terms, and by prepaid accounts
whose credit balance covers the order. If you would rather not contact us before ordering, add
enough credit to your account and place the order.

Accounts on manual invoicing order those products by contacting us. We confirm the scope, send an
invoice, and place the order on the account once the invoice is paid. Delivery starts after payment.

Postpaid terms are granted to accounts with an established history of paid invoices, and can be
requested through support.

We review orders and credit activity. An account whose activity does not look like normal business
use may be flagged or suspended. The account holder is informed when that happens and can contact
support to have the decision reviewed if it was made in error.

Bare metal servers are above the limit, so the rules above decide whether an account orders one
directly or through an invoice. Servers are subject to availability, have a one month minimum, and
carry a one-time setup fee.

## Products Available Today

Prices are in CAD and can change. The console shows the current price at checkout. The
[pricing page](https://zcp.zsoftly.ca/pricing) is the source of truth.

### Support

- Basic Support. Free and included with every account. Ticket portal support, 8 hours a day, 5 days
  a week, with best effort response and a 99.5% uptime SLA.
- Pro Support. $99 a month. Business hours support with a 4 hour P1 response and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

### Bare Metal Servers

The Bare Metal tab lists dedicated servers. Store product names take the form Bare Metal followed by
the CPU, for example Bare Metal AMD EPYC 4124P. Servers are listed in the Store tab. Most accounts
order them through the invoice-first path in How purchases are approved. A server bills monthly on
the same invoice as your instances. You choose the quantity. Prices are in CAD per month. Servers
are subject to availability, the minimum term is one month, and a one-time setup fee applies and is
shown at checkout.

## For Software Vendors

ZSoftly lists third-party products in the Store for its customers. ZSoftly handles billing and
first-line order handling, and pays the vendor a revenue share on each sale. To list a product,
reach us through the [contact form](https://zcp.zsoftly.ca/contact?source=docs&topic=partnership)
with the partnership topic selected.
